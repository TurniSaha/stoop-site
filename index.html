Stoop Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 11, 2026

What Stoop Collects

Stoop uses the photos you choose or take and the notes you type so it can clean up item photos and draft listing text for things you want to sell.

Where Your Data Goes

Your photos and notes go to your own Stoop agent, which you control. That agent sends photos to fal.ai for background removal and sends listing context to Anthropic to draft listing text.

What Stoop Does Not Do

  • No tracking.
  • No ads.
  • No analytics.
  • No sale of personal data.
  • No sharing with data brokers.

Local Network Use

Stoop connects to the Stoop agent running on your own computer or private network. The app stores the agent address and token on your device so it can reconnect.

Data Retention

The app and agent keep listing data only for the owner to manage their own selling workflow. You can delete items from the agent storage directly on the computer that runs it.
